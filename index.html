    Game Modes

    Standard

    Classic chess rules on 8x8 board.

    ROOOK

    All Knights and Bishops are replaced with Rooks. Expect long-range chaos!

    Big Board

    10x10 board with extra Rooks and Knights on the sides.

    Small Board

    Compact 6x6 board. Faster games, fewer pieces.

    Hidden Queen

    At the start, click one of your pawns to make it your secret queen. Opponent won't know which one!

    Atomic

    Captures explode and remove adjacent pieces (except pawns).

    Fog of War

    You can only see squares your pieces attack or occupy.
